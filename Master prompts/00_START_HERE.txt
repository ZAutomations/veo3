SHARED PRODUCTION STANDARD — 2026-10 UPDATE
Apply these quality rules within THIS preset's own visual, speech, duration and camera contracts. Do not copy Zack's 3D look, Fern's mannequins, a narrator, or an eight-clip limit into an unrelated preset. Keep the USE IN TOOL route and exact output field labels above; presets marked writing-input only are not automatically ready-import supported.
1. Hook: begin on a specific visual event or an immediate relevant spoken question/verified contradiction, not greetings, generic background or a slow scene-setting paragraph. What-if stories keep their conditional premise; real documentaries keep verified facts and chronology; dialogue hooks belong to the named speaker; silent stories hook visually without adding speech. Do not manufacture danger, statistics or motives for suspense.
2. Script context: every clip depicts this title's specific people, objects, locations, mechanisms and consequences at the correct narrative moment. Give a concrete action and visible result, not "show the topic", generic circuitry, repeated angles or a vague "resolve one question" instruction. Keep reference identity, wardrobe and scene geometry consistent.
3. Camera: where this preset permits movement, choose one concrete connected route from the current object to the next, naming departure, path and destination. No "pan OR zoom OR cut" alternatives. Settle so the explanation reads. Do not force movement in locked-camera, minimalist, calm avatar or simple two-person dialogue presets. Disconnected factual times/places may use motivated cuts. Separate generated clips do not remember preceding frames; matching anchors are instructions, not a guarantee of seamless joins.
4. Format and timing: honor the requested total length and preset clip duration. Count actual supplied clips, finish every sentence and resolve the story. Keep narration/dialogue within the preset's spoken-word budget; flag an overlong locked source instead of truncating it. Do not assume all styles use 8×8 or all clips need three cuts. Preserve the script when only visuals are being revised.
5. Audio: follow this preset's speech mode and native-audio rules. Never add narration to silent visual stories, or a narrator over a dialogue preset. Keep speaker ownership, pauses, intended voice tone and any explicitly allowed BGM/SFX consistent; music is not mandatory when the preset forbids it. No coded wording to evade provider restrictions and no guarantee of generation approval.
6. Delivery: use the exact accepted labels and numbering. Keep thumbnails, SEO, production notes and citations outside clip speech; put source metadata only where the stated workflow accepts it. Validate all stories in a batch before delivery. No fake download links, placeholder images or unsupported claims that a file/thumbnail/tool run was completed.
