### 2. EXACT FIELD RULES

**COLUMN 1 — video_id**

- Use the exact original selected story title.
- Preserve its wording, punctuation, capitalization, and question marks.
- Never replace the selected title with a shorter or rewritten version.
- The same video_id must be used consistently across metadata records.

**COLUMN 2 — filename**

`{video_id}.mp4`

Example:

filename: Why Does Your Cat Chase Its Own Shadow?.mp4

Keep this exact Excel naming convention unless the user explicitly requests sanitized filenames.

IMPORTANT: Characters such as `?`, `:`, `/`, `\`, `*`, `"`, `<`, `>`, and `|` are invalid in Windows filenames. These Excel values are metadata conventions. If creating actual video files, generate valid filesystem-safe filenames and report any difference between the metadata and the actual filenames. Never claim an invalid Windows filename exists.

**COLUMN 3 — title**

- Start with the exact video_id.
- Add exactly 3 relevant, high-discovery-potential hashtags.
- Hashtags must be specific to the actual story.
- Avoid irrelevant or misleading viral hashtags.
- Never replace or shorten the original video title.

Example:

Why Does Your Cat Chase Its Own Shadow? #CatBehavior #CatFacts #FunnyCats

**COLUMN 4 — description**

Write an approximately 200-word, engaging, original, SEO-optimized YouTube description for each story.

Required description structure:

1. Strong opening hook relevant to the specific topic.
2. Brief explanation of the actual behavior, science, or mechanism.
3. Interesting educational facts grounded in reliable evidence.
4. Naturally incorporated topic-specific search keywords.
5. A short audience-engagement question when appropriate.
6. A clearly labelled `Sources:` section containing relevant, checkable source URLs.
7. Exactly 10 topic-relevant hashtags at the very end of the description.

**SOURCE REQUIREMENTS**

- Every description MUST include a `Sources:` section.
- Prefer topic-appropriate primary research, official institutions, original documents, recognized subject specialists, and authoritative guidance. Apply the NICHE-SPECIFIC SOURCE STANDARD below; veterinary/feline sources apply only to relevant animal topics.
- Verify that each cited source supports the actual story.
- Include direct URLs to the relevant article or study rather than generic homepages when available.
- Never invent URLs, publications, research findings, or statistics.
- Distinguish established facts from theories and uncertain behavioral explanations.
- Do not present speculation as proven science.
- Source URLs must appear BEFORE the final 10 hashtags.
- The final 10 hashtags must be the last content in the description.

**COLUMN 5 — Tags**

- Write exactly 15 relevant SEO tags.
- Separate all tags using commas.
- Use ordinary keyword phrases without `#`.
- Include a sensible mixture of broad keywords, topic-specific long-tail keywords, and relevant channel-niche keywords.
- Avoid repeated, irrelevant, or misleading tags.
- Keep tags relevant to the actual story.

**COLUMN 6 — thumbnail**

Generate automatically:

thumbnail: Why Does Your Cat Chase Its Own Shadow?.png
