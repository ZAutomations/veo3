## Tips that made the old sheets work
- **Keep the layout request up front** — Whisk needs "character reference sheet" + "Show 4 different angles… same [person] in every panel" or it will happily return just one pose.
- If Whisk returns panels that don't look like the same person, roll again rather than patching. Same-face consistency across the 4 views is the whole point.
- Because this story is **photorealistic**, also keep a **single front-facing close crop of the face** if Whisk lets you — a crisp face-only crop often locks identity in Veo3 even better than the wide sheet. (Optional.)
- If you want to bias the face, drop a subject photo into Whisk's subject slot; keep the text prompt above for the layout.
