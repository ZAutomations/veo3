# Never Marry a Man Who Fears Your Silence - style bible

Preset: **Relationship Dialogue** (`relationship-dialogue`, niche)
Format: 16:9, 8s per clip, 7 clips, 56 seconds

## The look
Cinematic digital manhwa art style, soft 3D cel-shaded realism, 4k resolution, delicate soft lighting, atmospheric indoor ambient light, shallow depth of field, 35mm lens, smooth subtle facial movement, high aesthetic detail

## Cast template
Cinematic digital manhwa character, soft 3D cel-shaded semi-realistic rendering, realistic adult human proportions, delicate defined facial structure, detailed hair with individual strands, expressive but restrained eyes, contemporary or gently vintage clothing in fixed colours. NOT live-action, NOT a photograph, NOT 3D CGI, NOT western cartoon, NOT chibi.

## Palette
Honeyed afternoon light through gauze curtains, warm cream and porcelain white, muted sage green, dusty rose, deep teal shadow, soft gold highlights

## Camera
Wide two-shots to hold both people in the room, over-the-shoulder singles across the table, medium close-ups on the face that is listening rather than the one talking, insert shots on hands, teacups and folded letters, slow barely-there pushes

## Direction
This is a two-hander. Two people sit close in one calm, beautiful room - a cafe, a tearoom, a sunlit veranda, a library corner - and talk to each other. Everything said is said out loud by one of them to the other, in their own voice. There is no narrator and nobody describes the scene. Build the exchange as a hook that makes the viewer stop, then two or three numbered rules, then the younger one pushing back with the doubt the audience is actually feeling, then one sharp aphorism worth repeating, then a warm resolution. Give the listener something to do while the other speaks: a teacup turned, a hand stilled, an eye-line that drops and comes back. The camera favours the face that is listening. Let the warmth be earned by the truth of the advice, never by sentimentality.

## Voices
No narrator and no voice-over. The cast speak on screen, to each other,
and every word in the film comes out of one of their mouths.

## Script
- *A Warning Over Tea* — **Evelyn:** "Never marry a man who fears your silence, Clara." **Clara:** "He just gets so intense when I quiet down." **Evelyn:** "That intensity is a dangerous warning sign."
- *The First Rule* — **Evelyn:** "A healthy man views your quiet as peace." **Clara:** "And if he thinks I'm holding back?" **Evelyn:** "Then he sees a weapon where peace belongs."
- *The Second Rule* — **Evelyn:** "Love seeks to understand your stillness." **Evelyn:** "Insecurity demands a constant performance." **Clara:** "It feels so exhausting to perform."
- *The Granddaughter's Doubt* — **Clara:** "Isn't it because he cares too much?" **Clara:** "He just fears losing me, grandmother." **Evelyn:** "Fear is not an excuse for cruelty."
- *The Sharp Truth* — **Evelyn:** "Anger masking fear is still control." **Evelyn:** "It is never devotion, child." **Clara:** "I wanted so badly to save him."
- *A Moment of Clarity* — **Clara:** "I don't have to explain my quiet." **Evelyn:** "Your peace requires no apology."
- *Peace Restored* — **Evelyn:** "Wait for the man who rests inside your stillness." **Clara:** "I will, Grandmother." **Evelyn:** "You deserve a home, my darling, not a battlefield."
