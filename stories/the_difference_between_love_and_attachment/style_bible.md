## The look
Cinematic digital manhwa art style, soft 3D cel-shaded realism, 4k resolution, delicate soft lighting, atmospheric indoor ambient light, shallow depth of field, 35mm lens, smooth subtle facial movement, high aesthetic detail

## Cast template
Cinematic digital manhwa character, soft 3D cel-shaded semi-realistic rendering, realistic adult human proportions, delicate defined facial structure, detailed hair with individual strands, expressive but restrained eyes, contemporary or gently vintage clothing in fixed colours. NOT live-action, NOT a photograph, NOT 3D CGI, NOT western cartoon, NOT chibi.

## Palette
Honeyed afternoon light through gauze curtains, warm cream and porcelain white, muted sage green, dusty rose, deep teal shadow, soft gold highlights

## Camera
Wide two-shots to hold both people in the room, over-the-shoulder singles across the table, medium close-ups on the face that is listening rather than the one talking, insert shots on hands, teacups and folded letters, slow barely-there pushes

## Direction
This is a two-hander. Two people sit close together in one calm, beautiful room and talk to each other - the SAME room, the same table and the same light for the entire film. They never move to another place and the room never changes. Everything said is said out loud by one of them to the other, in their own voice. There is no narrator and nobody describes the scene. Build the exchange as a hook that makes the viewer stop, then two or three numbered rules, then the younger one pushing back with the doubt the audience is actually feeling, then one sharp aphorism worth repeating, then a warm resolution. Give the listener something to do while the other speaks: a teacup turned, a hand stilled, an eye-line that drops and comes back. The camera favours the face that is listening. Let the warmth be earned by the truth of the advice, never by sentimentality.

## Voices
No narrator and no voice-over. The cast speak on screen, to each other,
and every word in the film comes out of one of their mouths.

## Script
- *The Pain of Dependency* — **Kael:** "It hurts so much. Doesn't that mean I loved her?" **Elden:** "Pain does not measure love's depth, Kael. It measures dependency."
- *The Misconception of Grief* — **Kael:** "If it's just dependency... what was I feeling?" **Elden:** "You were feeling the terror of losing your mirror."
- *First Rule of Wholeness* — **Elden:** "First, remember this: love requires you to be whole first." **Kael:** "How can I be whole when I feel empty?"
- *The Nature of Clinging* — **Kael:** "I held on because I thought I was saving us." **Elden:** "You were clinging to save yourself, not the bond."
- *Second Rule of Freedom* — **Elden:** "Second rule: love holds open hands. Attachment grasps with fear." **Kael:** "To let go feels like giving up."
- *The Root of Fear* — **Elden:** "Fear of loneliness turns sweet affection into a cage." **Kael:** "I built the cage... didn't I?"
- *The Seeker's Doubt* — **Kael:** "But if it hurts this much... wasn't it true love?" **Elden:** "Pain doesn't measure love's depth, Kael." **Elden:** "It measures the depth of your dependency."
- *The Core Aphorism* — **Elden:** "Attachment says: 'I need you to complete me.'" **Elden:** "Love says: 'I am whole, and I walk beside you.'"
- *A Shift in Perspective* — **Kael:** "I was reaching for a piece of myself." **Elden:** "And expecting them to carry it."
- *Third Rule of Release* — **Elden:** "Release the outcome. Honor the warmth that remains." **Kael:** "Even if they never return?" **Elden:** "Especially then."
- *Reclaiming Personal Power* — **Kael:** "I can be whole... on my own." **Elden:** "You already are. You only forgot."
- *A Moment of Quiet* — **Elden:** "Walk forward, Kael. Gently." **Kael:** "Thank you, Elden."
- *Peaceful Resolution* — **Kael:** "I feel whole again." **Elden:** "You always were. You simply forgot." **Kael:** "I will choose peace now."
