# What If Earth Stopped Revolving Around the Sun - style bible

Preset: **Science What-If** (`science-what-if`, niche)
Format: 16:9, 8s per clip, 7 clips, 56 seconds

## The look
Photoreal scientific visualization, cinematic realism, dramatic natural lighting, immense scale, high-detail planetary and cosmic imagery, awe and wonder, premium streaming documentary quality

## Cast template
Realistic adult humans with realistic adult proportions, ordinary modern clothing suited to the environment, calm observational expressions, natural available light. NOT cartoon, NOT anime, NOT stylised.

## Palette
Deep ocean blues, molten ambers, atmospheric whites, cold stellar blacks, aurora greens

## Camera
Slow orbital reveals, vast establishing wides, gradual push-ins that land on one telling detail, scale-revealing crane moves

## Narrator
calm measured male voice, contemplative and unhurried, a quiet sense of scale

## Never
no named real scientists, universities or space agencies, no on-screen text, diagrams, labels or equations, no disaster-movie panic, screaming crowds or collapsing skylines, no pseudoscience, no invented physics, no glowing magic energy, no spiralling galaxies used as decoration

## Story shapes that suit this look
- one change happens, then the consequences unfold link by link
- the ordinary state, the moment it breaks, the world afterwards
- a familiar thing shown at a scale that makes it strange
- a calm explanation that keeps getting more extreme

## Cast
None. This topic is about the world rather than a person, so no
character sheets were written and no Characters need to be uploaded
into Flow before stage 1. Distant unnamed figures are scenery.

## What happens next
1. No reference sheets and no Characters to upload - skip straight to stage 1.

3. Then the usual stages 2-4.
