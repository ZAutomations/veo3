folder under the name given below.

tab. A Flow Character is re-invented for every clip, which is what made
faces and wardrobes drift from cut to cut. A plain image mentioned with
@Name is handed to the model as reference pixels and holds. Stage 2 does

angles. Keep it to one file per character: the video model accepts at
most 3 reference images, and the place plate takes one of those slots -
so a cast of 2 or 3 is the ceiling.
